## 📖 Overview
This report explains **Newton’s Universal Law of Gravitation** and **Kepler’s Third Law**, including derivations and applications. It shows how these laws govern planetary motion, satellite orbits, and space exploration.

---

## 🔹 Key Topics
- **Gravitation:** Force of attraction between masses  
- **Newton’s Law:** \( F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2} \)  
- **Kepler’s Third Law:** \( T^2 \propto R^3 \)  
- **Derivation:** From Newton’s Law to Kepler’s Third Law  
- **Applications:** Satellites, planetary motion, space missions  

---

## 🚀 Applications
- Predicting planetary and satellite orbits  
- Launching rockets and planning space missions  
- Understanding motion in the universe

## ✅ Conclusion
Gravitation and Kepler’s laws are fundamental in physics and astronomy. They help explain and predict the movement of planets, moons, satellites, and spacecraft.
